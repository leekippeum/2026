How to Apply

TRAINING PROGRAMMES

Step 1.

Register on the Official Website
Visit the official ROK-ICAO FTP website and create an account.
Log in to access the application portal.

Step 2.

Select a Training Course & Submit the Application Browse the list of available training courses.
Click on your desired course and complete the online application form.
Upload the required documents:
Official Nomination Form (Attachment C)
Medical Report

Step 3.

Selection Process & Announcement
The selection process will be conducted based on the submitted applications.
Once the selection is finalized, the list of selected trainees will be posted on the official website after ICAO confirmation.

Step 4.

ICAO Award & Pre-Departure Preparation
Selected trainees will receive an award from ICAO.
The designated training institution (CATC, IIAC, etc.) will contact each trainee individually with further instructions.
Trainees should prepare for their travel and entry into Korea following the provided guidelines.

Remarks

Selections will be made by the Selection Committee in the Republic of Korea with the application forms, state letters from its Aviation Authorities, and official nomination form that are uploaded to the website (www.korea-ftp.org).

For more details, please visit the official ROK-ICAO FTP website.
